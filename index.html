Who We Are

Built by an African,
For Africans in Egypt.

Xairod was born from a real problem. As a Nigerian student in Cairo, finding trusted health services, African food, safe housing and a reliable admission agency was harder than it should be. So we built the solution.

🌍
The Mission
To make Cairo feel like home for every African — whether you're a student, professional or family. Trusted services, real community, zero confusion.
🏢
The Company
Xairod is operated by USAFA Ltd, registered in England & Wales, and its Nigerian operating entity Xairod LLC. We are proudly Nigerian-founded.
✅
The Promise
Every business on Xairod is manually verified. Health providers are licensed. Agencies are KYC-checked. You connect only with real, vetted services.
