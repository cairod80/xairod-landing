This Privacy Policy applies to all users of xairod.com and business.xairod.com. It explains what data we collect, why, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

The Xairod platform is operated by USAFA Ltd, registered in England and Wales. For data protection purposes, USAFA Ltd is the data controller. Our Nigerian operating entity is Xairod LLC, registered with the CAC of Nigeria.

Contact: hello@xairod.com

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account on the Xairod platform
  • To match customer requests with appropriate service providers
  • To process transactions, hold funds in escrow, and issue payouts
  • To send transaction confirmations, notifications and service updates
  • To send WhatsApp automation messages related to your requests (with your consent)
  • To monitor platform safety and detect fraud or policy violations
  • To comply with legal obligations including anti-money laundering requirements
  • To improve platform features using anonymised analytics

5. KYC Document Storage

Business partner KYC documents (passports, licences) are stored in a private encrypted Supabase Storage bucket with no public URLs. Only authorised Xairod administrators can access them via time-limited signed URLs (60 minutes). Documents are never displayed publicly.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and Nigeria NDPR you have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, receive a portable copy, and object to processing. Email hello@xairod.com to exercise any of these rights. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

  • HTTPS encryption on all connections
  • Passwords hashed — never stored in plain text
  • Row-Level Security on Supabase database
  • Payment data handled exclusively by PCI-compliant Paystack
  • API keys stored as server-side environment variables only

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies only — required for authentication sessions and platform functionality. We do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics cookies that follow you across other websites.

10. Children's Privacy

Xairod is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will delete their account immediately.

11. Changes

Material changes will be communicated via email and in-app notification at least 14 days before they take effect.
